Rich McCue

Digital Scholarship Commons Manager, UVic Libraries
Instructor, UVic Faculty of Education
rmccue@uvic.ca

I manage the University of Victoria Libraries Digital Scholarship Commons (DSC). I am also a sessional instructor in the Faculty of Education, where I teache courses on Educational Technology (EDCI 336), Technology Innovation in Indigenous Education (IED 336), and Multimedia and Online Learning (EDCI 337). The Digital Scholarship Commons (DSC) helps the UVic community explore and express their ideas in ways other than text and tell their research stories in interesting and engaging ways. My research interests include generative AI in education, flipped learning, makerspace pedagogy, informal credentialing, and open learning. Rich has presented at many academic technology and makerspace conferences around the world. In his spare time, he reads, woodwork’s, plays soccer, travels & bikes.
